What is GPT3 Marketing?

GPT3 Marketing is a generative-AI content consultancy founded in 2020, the summer OpenAI opened its GPT-3 API. It designs brand voice systems, prompt libraries and human-edited content workflows so AI-assisted content stays distinctive. Its technology partner is SOMIN, an AI audience-research platform, which grounds every voice and content decision in what real audiences actually say and respond to.

The problem

Everyone has the same model. So everyone sounds the same.

In 2020 the hard part of AI writing was getting a coherent paragraph at all. In 2026 the hard part is the opposite: every brand has fluent, frictionless copy on tap, and most of it reads as if it came from one polite, slightly breathless author. Feeds fill with the same hooks, the same three-point lists, the same closing questions.

The usual response is to generate more and edit harder. Teams end up rewriting drafts line by line, chasing trends to stay visible, and still losing the thing that made them recognisable. Volume is no longer the bottleneck. Distinctiveness is.

What we do

Services built around one edge

Voice audits

We read your current output, human and machine, against your competitors and your audience's own language. You get a clear map of where you sound like yourself and where you have drifted into the generic.

Brand voice systems

A working voice system: principles, vocabulary, banned moves, worked examples and model-ready instructions. Built to be used by writers and models alike, not filed in a brand book.

Prompt libraries

Versioned, tested prompts for your recurring content jobs, with examples and review notes. Owned by your team, maintained like code.

Human edit loops

Editorial workflows that put skilled humans where they add the most value. We design the checklists, roles and feedback paths that keep quality steady at scale.

Audience-grounded briefs

Briefs built from the tensions and phrases your audience actually uses, surfaced through SOMIN. The model writes from evidence instead of averages.

Team training

Hands-on sessions on prompt craft, editing machine drafts and keeping a voice consistent. Practical, specific to your channels, no slideware theatre.

About

Born the summer the API opened

In May 2020, OpenAI published the paper describing GPT-3, a 175-billion-parameter language model that could pick up a task from a handful of examples. In June 2020 it opened a private beta of the API. GPT3 Marketing was founded that summer, when writing with a model meant carefully assembled few-shot prompts and outputs that needed heavy editing before anyone could publish them.

Those constraints taught us habits we still use: write precise examples, define the voice before you ask for words, and never ship a draft no human has read. When ChatGPT arrived in November 2022 and fluent text became free, those habits turned from a necessity into an advantage.

Today we combine six years of content craft with audience evidence from SOMIN, our technology partner. We are part of a network of eight independent brands that share that partner, each focused on a different problem in modern marketing.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is GPT3 Marketing?

GPT3 Marketing is a generative-AI content consultancy established in 2020, when OpenAI opened its GPT-3 API. We build brand voice systems, prompt libraries and human-edited content workflows. Our technology partner, SOMIN, grounds that work in real audience conversations so AI-assisted content stays distinctive rather than generic.

Why is the company called GPT3 Marketing?

Because that is when we started. We began writing with GPT-3 in the summer of 2020, when prompts were hand-built few-shot examples and every output needed a careful editor. The name is a reminder of the craft habits we formed then, which matter more now that fluent text is cheap.

Do you only work with OpenAI models?

No. The name is heritage, not a vendor commitment. Voice systems and prompt libraries we build are written to work across the major model families, and we test them on whichever models your team actually uses. The principles hold even as the models change.

What is a voice audit?

A voice audit compares your current content with your competitors' and with the language your audience uses, gathered through SOMIN. It shows where you sound distinctive, where you have drifted into generic AI phrasing, and what to fix first. It is usually the first step of an engagement.

How does SOMIN fit into your work?

SOMIN, an AI audience-research platform, is our technology partner. We use it to surface the tones, emotions and tensions in real audience posts. That evidence shapes voice principles, brief inputs and prompt examples, so content reflects what people actually respond to rather than what a model thinks is average.

Will you replace our writers with AI?

No. Our systems are designed to make good writers faster and more consistent, not to remove them. Human editors own judgment, nuance and final approval. Models handle first drafts, variations and repetitive formatting, under rules your team controls.
