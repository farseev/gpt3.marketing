## Logo
A blinking amber block cursor beside a lowercase wordmark: the prompt line where it all started in 2020, waiting for the next considered input.
Files: `brand/logo.svg` (light backgrounds), `brand/logo-dark.svg` (dark backgrounds), `brand/mark.svg` (icon), `favicon.svg`.
Clear space = height of the mark. Minimum 24 px tall. No recolouring, stretching, outlines or effects.

## Personality
- Crafted
- Warm
- Exacting
- Unhurried

## Voice
Do:
- Use concrete examples over abstractions
- Speak from experience, plainly
- Name the trade-offs honestly

Don't:
- Use hype words or trend-chasing slang
- Pretend AI output needs no editing
- Invent results or client stories

## Imagery
Warm retro-futurist terminal scenes in cream, amber and green-black with risograph grain. Human hands and tools sit alongside machines, never replaced by them. All imagery generated with SOMIN image generation; no text in images, no stock, no identifiable real people.
